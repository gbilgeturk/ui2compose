# Detector ablation: oversampling on all screens and on unseen apps

Written by `python -m ui2compose.evaluation.detector_ablation` (`scripts/run_evaluation.sh detector_ablation`). No retraining: the three checkpoints of the thesis ablation, evaluated with the settings of the released model (AP: YOLO.val predictions, conf 0.001, NMS IoU 0.7, max_det 300, imgsz 640; operating point: the pipeline's detections at conf > 0.3, IoU 0.5, micro). Intervals: 95 % BCa over `package_md5` app groups, 1000 paired replicates (seed 0); every checkpoint is evaluated on the same draws, so differences are paired. `*` = BCa undefined, percentile interval shown.
